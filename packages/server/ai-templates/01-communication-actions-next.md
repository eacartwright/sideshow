# Communication / Actions Taken / Next Steps

Three sections, in this order, each a bold heading on its own line followed by "* " bullets:

**Communication**
Who contacted the technician or who was contacted or updated by the technician, how, and what was said or agreed.

**Actions Taken**
What was done, in the order it was done: checks, findings, changes, results.

**Next Steps**
What's left to do, and who is doing it.

Leave out a section with nothing in it.
